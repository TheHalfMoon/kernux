# Kernux Code of Conduct

Kernux contributors, maintainers, reviewers, users, and automated agents are expected to keep project interactions safe, professional, and focused on useful technical outcomes.

## Expected conduct

- Treat people with respect across differences in experience, background, identity, language, and viewpoint.
- Critique code, evidence, decisions, and behavior rather than attacking people.
- Give review feedback that is specific, reproducible, and proportionate to the risk.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, security-reporting boundaries, and contributor attribution.
- Make room for questions and corrections without using uncertainty as a reason to fabricate evidence.
- Follow repository governance even when a shortcut would be faster.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate exposure of private information, sustained personal attacks, bad-faith disruption, impersonation, evidence fabrication, or attempts to pressure others into bypassing security/governance are not acceptable.
## Scope and reporting

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, project-managed community spaces, and other interactions where a person is representing Kernux.

Use GitHub's reporting or moderation controls for platform abuse when applicable. If project-maintainer action is needed and no private conduct-reporting channel is published, open a minimal issue requesting a private channel; do not post sensitive personal details in that issue.

Maintainers may remove content, request correction, warn participants, restrict participation, or block contributors when necessary to protect the project or community. Enforcement should consider severity, repetition, context, safety, and the need to avoid unnecessary disclosure.

Retaliation against good-faith reporters, reviewers, or participants is not acceptable. Knowingly false reports or deliberate manipulation of the enforcement process may themselves violate this policy.

## Policy provenance

This is original Kernux project policy. It does not copy or incorporate an external code-of-conduct template. Changes to it are governed repository changes and require ordinary review/evidence rather than unilateral agent or contributor edits.
